Hi, I'm Peter Muchai!

Senior software engineer / Distributed systems & AI platforms

I'm a software engineer with 13+ years of experience turning complex requirements into dependable systems. My work spans digital banking, payments and Microsoft 365, connecting architecture, hands-on implementation and the realities of running software in production.

At Microsoft, where I joined in March 2021, I build the data foundations behind enterprise search and Copilot. My work spans ingestion-time enrichment and asynchronous backfill, tenant provisioning and quota management, relationship extraction, and semantic labeling. The design challenge is not simply moving more data: it is choosing what deserves processing, preserving permissions and schema contracts, and keeping a worldwide service reliable as it evolves.

I design for correctness under load, bounded resource consumption and recoverable failures. I've worked on shard-aware routing, bounded concurrency and authentication migrations, and used end-to-end traces to separate source delays from queueing and service time. My work on quality-gated processing reduced unnecessary memory, compute and storage use without sacrificing ranking quality. More recently, I built AI-assisted diagnostics to connect evidence across services and make missing-content investigations faster and more repeatable.

Before Microsoft, I built and operated banking and payment platforms at iCube Fintech and Finserve. Working across core banking, mobile money and payment networks taught me to make transaction state explicit, isolate provider differences behind stable APIs, and design for timeouts, retries and downstream failure. Security was part of the contract: OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, signed JWTs, least-privilege scopes and short-lived credentials.

I'm hands-on in C#/.NET, Java, Kotlin and SQL, with experience across Azure, event-driven services, relational and distributed data stores, and containerized delivery. I lead through design reviews, clear interface contracts, practical mentoring and close work with product, compliance and operations. I take responsibility from the first design discussion through rollout, incident response and handover. What matters to me is a system whose behavior people can explain, whose failures they can diagnose, and whose next change they can make safely.
